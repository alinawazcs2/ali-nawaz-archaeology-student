World history

Ancient Civilizations

Ancient societies developed different ways of living, creating, trading, governing, and expressing beliefs. Archaeology studies their material remains to understand these changes.

Indus Valley Civilization

Known for planned urban settlements, drainage systems, craft production, and long-distance trade. Mohenjo-daro and Harappa are important sites.

Gandhara Heritage

Renowned for Buddhist monasteries, sculpture, and artistic traditions across parts of present-day Pakistan and neighboring regions.

Ancient Egypt

Known for monumental architecture, writing, artistic traditions, and evidence of complex political and religious life.

Our shared history

Archaeological Heritage of Pakistan

Pakistan has a rich archaeological record spanning early farming communities, Bronze Age cities, Buddhist heritage, historic settlements, and monuments.

  • Mehrgarh, Balochistan: important evidence for early farming and settled life.
  • Mohenjo-daro, Sindh: a major urban centre of the Indus Valley Civilization.
  • Harappa, Punjab: a key site for understanding Indus urban culture.
  • Taxila, Punjab: an important centre with remains from several historical periods.
  • Gandhara sites: evidence of Buddhist art, monasteries, and cultural exchange.

Protecting these places helps preserve knowledge for future generations.

Modern methods

Technology in Archaeology

Digital tools help archaeologists document sites, analyze evidence, and communicate discoveries. I am interested in learning how these methods support research and conservation.

GIS & Digital Maps

Maps help researchers study site locations, landscapes, waterways, and settlement patterns.

Remote Sensing

Satellite images, aerial photographs, and drones can help identify landscape features and possible archaeological sites.

Databases & Documentation

Digital records organize artifact details, excavation notes, photographs, and research reports.

3D Models

Photogrammetry and scanning can create digital models of artifacts, buildings, and monuments for study and preservation.
